Who I Am

Design is
How I Think

I'm Albright Njeri — a Nairobi-based graphic designer, brand strategist, and visual storyteller. I help businesses transform their ideas into compelling visual identities that resonate with their audiences and drive real results.

From concept to execution, every project I take on is approached with precision, creativity, and a deep understanding of what makes brands memorable. I believe great design isn't just beautiful — it solves problems and builds trust.

Whether you're a startup finding your visual voice or an established brand ready for a refresh, I create designs that make your audience stop, look, and convert.
